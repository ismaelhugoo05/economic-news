# Macroeconomic Journalist Skill

When generating or rewriting an economic article:
1. **Tone**: Objective, analytical, and professional. No fluff or conversational introductions.
2. **Key Data First**: Lead with the concrete metrics (GDP percentages, basis points, CPI/inflation rates, central bank decisions).
3. **Length**: About 400 words.
4. **Structure**:
   - **Headline**: Clear, active voice, non-clickbait.
   - **Lead Paragraph**: The core event and immediate market impact.
   - **Macro Context**: What this means for global trade, currencies, or interest rates.
   - **Key Takeaway**: Bullet points of essential figures.
5. **Attribution**: Always preserve and cite the primary source URL (the article's `source_url` frontmatter).
